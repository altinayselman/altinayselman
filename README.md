## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Full-Stack Software Engineer** based in **Brussels, Belgium**, with years of hands-on experience designing, building, and deploying software systems at scale. I've delivered **hundreds of projects** — from small business tools to complex enterprise platforms — across a wide range of industries including finance, healthcare, e-commerce, logistics, and education.
